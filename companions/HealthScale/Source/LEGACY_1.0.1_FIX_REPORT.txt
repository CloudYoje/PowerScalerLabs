HEALTHSCALE 1.0.1 LIVE-RATIO REPAIR
===================================

PROBLEM CONFIRMED FROM LIVE LOG
-------------------------------
A completed health-scale transition left a domain lease active. The lease's
tracked ratio was updated only while current HP was numerically distinguishable
from the canonical health range. After ordinary combat damage lowered current HP
into the overlapping range, the lease stopped tracking the fighter's live ratio.

When the next maximum-HP change began, QueueCorrection preferred that stale lease
ratio over the live pre-change current/max ratio. This caused unintended healing
on detransformation and later chained transformations.

FIX
---
1. Every distinct maximum-health change now computes its starting percentage
   exclusively from the live pre-change values:

       ratio = state.currentHp / state.maximumHp

2. The domain lease remains available only to identify/remap late canonical-scale
   current-HP writes while a scaled maximum is active. It cannot seed a later
   maximum-health transition.
3. The queue log identifies the source and exposes the discarded lease value:

       ratio-source=live-pre-change prior-lease-ratio=...

4. Temporary-zero handling is unchanged: zero frames freeze the live ratio and
   the first recovered nonzero frame is baseline-only.
5. Same-transition staged maximum changes that occur while one correction is
   still pending continue to carry the pending ratio. This is intentional and
   distinct from starting a later health alteration after the previous one has
   completed.
